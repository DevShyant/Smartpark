# 🚗 SmartPark – Integrated Smart Mobility Platform

**Park Smart. Move Freely. Charge Green.**

## 📌 About the Project

SmartPark is an integrated smart mobility platform designed to make urban transportation more convenient, accessible, and sustainable. It brings three essential mobility services together in one application: **smart parking reservations, vehicle rentals, and EV charging station discovery**.

Instead of switching between multiple applications, users can find and book nearby parking spaces, rent cars, bikes, and scooters, and locate compatible EV charging stations from a single platform.

SmartPark connects customers with parking space owners, vehicle rental providers, and EV charging operators, creating a convenient digital ecosystem while opening new business opportunities for service providers.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Urban commuters face several mobility challenges:

- Difficulty finding available parking spaces in crowded areas.
- Time wasted searching for parking and comparing prices.
- Vehicle rental services scattered across different platforms.
- Difficulty locating nearby EV charging stations.
- Limited information about charging speeds, connector types, prices, and availability.
- Lack of a unified platform for everyday mobility services.

## 💡 Our Solution

SmartPark addresses these challenges through a single, user-friendly platform that integrates parking, vehicle rentals, and EV charging discovery.

Users can select their required service, explore nearby options, compare prices and details, and complete bookings through a simple workflow.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🅿️ Smart Parking
- Discover nearby parking spaces.
- Search by location, area, or landmark.
- Compare parking prices and availability.
- Book parking on an hourly, daily, or monthly basis.
- View booking details and confirmation.

### 🚘 Vehicle Rentals
- Rent cars, bikes, and scooters.
- Browse available vehicles and compare prices.
- Filter vehicles based on category and rental requirements.
- Select pickup and return dates.
- Manage rental bookings.

### ⚡ EV Charging Station Locator
- Discover nearby EV charging stations.
- View station locations and directions.
- Check supported connector types.
- Compare charging speeds and pricing.
- View real-time availability where operator integration is supported.

### 👤 User Dashboard
- User registration and login.
- Manage profile information.
- View all bookings in one place.
- Track booking status and history.
- Manage eligible cancellations.

### 🏢 Service Provider Dashboard
- Manage parking listings and availability.
- Add and manage rental vehicles.
- Update pricing and service details.
- View and manage customer bookings.

### 🛠️ Admin Dashboard
- Manage users and service providers.
- Monitor platform listings.
- Manage bookings.
- View platform activity and statistics.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Open SmartPark:** Register, log in, and provide location access.
2. **Select a Service:** Choose Parking, Vehicle Rental, or EV Charging.
3. **Explore Options:** Browse nearby services and available listings.
4. **View Details & Confirm:** Compare location, price, duration, and availability.
5. **Booking Confirmation:** Receive booking details, booking ID, and navigation information.

## 🌟 What Makes SmartPark Unique?

- Combines three major mobility services in one platform.
- Simplifies urban parking and vehicle rental discovery.
- Makes EV charging information easier to access.
- Connects customers and local service providers.
- Offers a unified booking experience.
- Supports the vision of connected and sustainable urban transportation.

## 💰 Business Model

SmartPark has the potential to generate revenue through:

- Commission on parking reservations.
- Commission on vehicle rental bookings.
- Partnerships and revenue sharing with EV charging operators.
- Subscription plans for service providers.
- Future Smart Mobility Hub partnerships.

## 🚀 Future Scope

Our future vision is to develop **Smart Mobility Hubs** that combine parking facilities, vehicle rentals, and EV charging infrastructure at integrated physical locations.

Planned enhancements include:

- AI-based parking availability predictions.
- Live EV charging station integration.
- QR-based parking entry and exit.
- GPS-based rental vehicle tracking.
- Advanced route and mobility recommendations.
- Expansion to more cities and service providers.

## 🎯 Project Vision

To create a connected, intelligent, and sustainable mobility ecosystem that simplifies everyday transportation by bringing parking, rentals, and EV charging together on one platform.
